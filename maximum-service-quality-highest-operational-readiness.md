# Maximum service quality & highest operational readiness

The simulator will be used again for training next year. This is because the new PANTHER 6x6 is due to be delivered at the end of 2025, for which the members of the airport fire brigade will, of course, be intensively prepared. In 2026, another new vehicle will be added to the fleet: a rescue extinguishing vehicle with a 4,500-liter water tank, also from Rosenbauer, of course.

The airport fire brigade comprises around 70 crew members, 15 of whom are employed full-time. The other colleagues pursue other occupations during ongoing airport operations and are called up in the event of an emergency. In an emergency, they are alerted via pager. The maximum response time (from alert to arrival at the scene) is only two to three minutes.
